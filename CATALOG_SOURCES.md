# Product names and specification references

The catalog uses real product names with a small subset of published specifications.
All game prices, stock, delivery times and faults are invented training data.
The meshes, icons and UI are original simplified representations, not manufacturer
CAD files, product photographs, logos or exact replicas. Brand names identify
components; the game is not affiliated with or endorsed by these manufacturers.

The GTX 1650 entry is specifically the GIGABYTE WINDFORCE GDDR5 model with a
6-pin auxiliary power connection. Other GTX 1650 cards may have different memory
or power connectors. This prototype models connecting a lead, but does not yet
model keyed 6/8-pin connector geometry or PSU cable selection.

DDR4-3200 is the memory's rated specification, not a promise that every CPU/board
combination runs it at that speed by default. CPU support, BIOS versions, XMP,
memory QVLs, electrical limits and case clearances are not fully simulated.
The game's single service DIMM slot and two case screws simplify real assemblies.
CPU, board, PSU, cooler, fan and case products are catalog/inspection-only in this
build. For actual repairs, verify the exact part revision and manufacturer support.
