# Readability, customers, white parts and RGB

Extract and run **Start_Windows.bat**. Existing saves are supported; finish your current ticket to enter the new customer rotation. Accepted builds keep their agreed budget and previous component prices.

## Readability

Larger high-contrast tool labels, a dedicated higher-resolution world font, 16x anisotropic filtering and 1080p physical monitor textures improve clarity. Texture padding is handled correctly on drivers that require it. Four-sample MSAA is requested where supported.

Hold **C** while walking to focus on distant objects and labels; release to restore your FOV. Use **E** at a monitor for its full-screen desktop. **F11** uses native fullscreen. Tiny writing cannot remain legible at arbitrary distances, so focus or approach it when necessary.

## Customer requests

In a new game, ticket 2 is a whole-PC gaming build and ticket 3 is an office repair. Six of every sixteen requests are full builds. Four repair types use office configurations. No three consecutive tickets are repair-only.

A build customer arrives without a PC. At reception, ask what they will use the PC for, then ask their budget and preferences. Agree to receive the parts deposit and start from an empty workbench. Office budgets range from $650–$1000, gaming $1200–$2000, and editing/study/gaming $1800–$2400.

The first $2000 gaming request asks for 32 GB RAM, 1 TB storage, RTX 5060 Ti 16 GB class graphics, white components and RGB. The budget is a ceiling: unused money is refunded and labour is paid separately. All nine budget/usage combinations have affordable solutions in the catalog.

Order and collect parts; fit the case, PSU, motherboard, CPU, paste, cooler, RAM, storage, GPU when needed, and fan. Connect cables, close and secure the case, connect power, test and sign off. Carry the finished tower to collection. The optional J → Custom build requests shortcut remains available before accepting a repair request.

Office PCs have integrated graphics, no dedicated GPU, no RGB and a distinct plain solid-panel case. Their panel opens for repairs. NPCs now carry the actual assembled computer on arrival and collection, with its matching internal components and lighting style.

## White parts and RGB

Fourteen new catalog entries bring the marketplace to 83 products. White fans, graphics cards, coolers, RAM kits, a PSU and case are available, alongside the office case and a 2 TB SSD. Use the shop's **All finishes / White only** button. White build requests preselect the filter for relevant component categories.

RGB uses soft local diffusers on RGB-capable parts. Cycle the build's RGB button through **Off → Aurora → Ice → Warm white**. Settings can disable RGB globally. Ordinary fans and RAM do not gain imaginary LEDs. Lighting can be previewed while assembling with mains disconnected. The Corsair LINK fan's controller requirement is abstracted into installation. Memory kits are one inventory item but show two modules.

These are simplified original procedural models, not manufacturer CAD models. See **PRICE_REFERENCE.md** for pricing references and **verification/VALIDATION.md** for checks and platform limitations.
